---

Social SDK gives a TypeScript application one typed model for connected accounts, content, media, publications, and destination outcomes. Choose a direct platform adapter when your application owns credentials and authorization, or use a managed backend when its account and operation coverage fits your requirements.

Start with [installation](/getting-started/installation), compare the [platform support matrix](/platforms), and read [the integration model](/concepts/integration-model) before choosing an adapter.

The [reference section](/reference) documents the public contracts and capability evidence currently available. The SDK does not provide a hosted social service, credential proxy, queue, database, or scheduler.
